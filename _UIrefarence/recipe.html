Spicy Vegetable Stir-fry

A vibrant, nutrient-dense stir-fry featuring a balanced heat from Thai chilies and the crisp texture of farm-fresh seasonal vegetables. Perfectly portioned for your current inventory.

schedule 15 Minutes
restaurant Medium Effort
local_fire_department 420 kcal

Ingredients Used

check_circle
Broccoli Florets
200g
check_circle
Bell Peppers (Red)
1 Unit
check_circle
Soy Sauce
2 Tbsp
check_circle
Firm Tofu
150g
error
Sesame Oil
MISSING

Pro Tip

Add a pinch of toasted sesame seeds at the very end to mimic the missing oil's nutty profile.

Step-by-Step

1

Prep & Sear Tofu

Pat the firm tofu dry and cut into 1-inch cubes. Heat a large skillet with a drizzle of cooking oil and sear the tofu until golden brown on all sides. Set aside.

2

The Aromatics

In the same pan, add minced ginger and garlic. Sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant, then toss in the broccoli and sliced bell peppers. Keep the heat high for a proper char.

3

Glaze & Finish

Pour in the soy sauce and a splash of water to create a light steam. Toss the tofu back in and mix thoroughly until the sauce thickens and coats the vegetables.
