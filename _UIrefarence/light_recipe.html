Spinach & Tomato Mediterranean Salad

A crisp, refreshing salad that comes together in minutes. Perfect for a light lunch or as a vibrant side dish, featuring a simple lemon-olive oil dressing.

schedule

Prep Time

15 min

group

Servings

2

Ingredients

Dressing

Instructions

1

Prepare the Greens

Thoroughly wash the baby spinach and spin dry. If the spinach leaves are particularly large, you may roughly chop them. Place the prepared spinach into a large serving bowl.

2

Chop Vegetables

Halve the cherry tomatoes, dice the cucumber, and thinly slice the red onion. Add these chopped vegetables to the bowl with the spinach.

3

Whisk the Dressing

In a small separate bowl or jar, combine the extra virgin olive oil, fresh lemon juice, a pinch of salt, and freshly ground black pepper. Whisk or shake vigorously until well emulsified.

4

Toss and Serve

Drizzle the dressing over the salad right before serving. Toss gently to ensure all leaves are lightly coated. Top with crumbled feta cheese or toasted pine nuts if desired.
